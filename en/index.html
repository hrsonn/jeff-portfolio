Projects and Results

Completed 2024

Anode Effect Reduction (FDEA)

Strategic project developed in primary aluminum production focusing on reducing the frequency and duration of anode effects, one of the sector's main operational challenges.

The initiative involved implementing improvements in process control systems, operational monitoring, team training, and optimization of electrolytic cell parameters. As a result, significant gains were achieved in energy efficiency, operational stability, and production performance, along with reduced greenhouse gas emissions (PFCs).

The project contributed to energy consumption reduction, lower operational costs, and alignment with international best practices in the aluminum industry, becoming an internal benchmark for continuous improvement and operational excellence.
